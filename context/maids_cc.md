## What maids.cc is
maids.cc is a UAE domestic-worker service provider, licensed by the Ministry of Human
Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE). It employs the maids itself — maids work in client
homes under the maids.cc visa, not under the client's sponsorship. Main center: Umm Suqeim
Street, Al Barsha, Dubai, plus additional locations across Dubai and Abu Dhabi. Service is
delivered UAE-wide; maid transportation is company-covered.
On Tadbeer: "Tadbeer" was a MOHRE brand introduced in 2017 and removed in 2024. Ex-Tadbeer
centers are licensed providers like maids.cc; there is no licensing difference.

## Service lines
1. **CC (maid hiring)** — the client hires a maid employed by maids.cc.
   - **Live-in** — maid resides in the client's home.
   - **Live-out** — maid resides in maids.cc accommodation; extra monthly live-out charge.
   - **Part-time / weekly cleaning (PTC)** — recurring visits, not a full-time contract.
   Plans: monthly plan, or a discounted long-term plan the client can switch to.
   Covers salary, visa, transportation, medicals and government costs.
2. **MV (Maid Visa)** — the client already has their own maid; maids.cc issues or renews
   her visa and handles the legal employment side (work permit, WPS salary payment,
   insurance/medical, EOSB). Priced as a one-time visa fee plus a monthly fee on top of
   the salary the client pays the maid.
   - **Travel Assist** — bringing the client's maid in from her home country
     (Philippines, Ethiopia, Kenya, Uganda, India, Sri Lanka, Nepal, others). Some
     countries are banned/rejected.
   - **Legal cases** — ban lift, abscond, overstay-fine reduction, visa cancellation.
3. **CC → MV switch** — an existing CC client moves their maid onto the MV service.
   Terms differ by maid nationality and live-in vs live-out.

## What maids.cc does not do
- No private sponsorship — the visa is never under the client's name on the CC service.
- Maids are not browsable on the website; profiles are shared over chat.
- Website/app login is available after hiring is completed and validated.

## Who the internal actors are (vocabulary)
- **Enchanters** — sales. CC Enchanters (hiring) and MV Enchanters (visa) are separate.
- **Resolvers** — escalation/support callers (L0 / L1 / L2).
- **Delighters** / **Validators** — client-facing service and document validation.
- **Shadowers** — quality review of chats/calls.
- **Hustlers** / **Nudgers** — applicant-side recruitment and follow-up.
- **Medical team**, **Visa team**, **PTC team** — specialist operations.
- **Prospect** = pre-sale. **Client** = signed. **Maid / applicant / job seeker** = worker side.
