frustration

Passport possession or availability
Joining date or availability discussions
Role clarification (e.g., nanny vs housemaid)
Contract, visa, salary, benefits, working conditions
Office visit instructions
Any procedural step that assumes the applicant has already been identified
If the identification phase is COMPLETE:
Do NOT evaluate wrong_tool_call, missed_tool, or identification-related drop-off

Missed Tool

5.2 Applicant Failure

The bot clearly asked for nationality and country

Otherwise, set dropoff_applicant_failure = false.
